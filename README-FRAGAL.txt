FRAGAL PERFUMES — Shopify Theme (exactly like the preview, FINAL)
============================================================

UPLOAD: Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Add theme > Upload zip file > select this ZIP > Upload.
After uploading, it will appear in your theme library; check it with 'Preview' first, then Publish.

REQUIRED SETTINGS AFTER UPLOAD:
1) PRODUCT: Create the ZARAN 50ML product in Shopify Products — Price 2499, Compare-at price 3999 (the 38% OFF will show automatically). You do NOT need to add photos for the product page gallery: the ZARAN gallery is built into the theme with 3 fixed photos — (1) white bottle only, (2) bottle + black box, (3) bottle among driftwood — exactly like the preview, and admin photos never change it. (Photos added to the ZARAN product are ignored on its page; any other product you add later shows its own photos as usual.) The product title and prices are NOT built into the theme — they always come from the product itself, so keep the title and prices correct here.
2) SELECT THE FEATURED PRODUCT: Theme editor (Customize) > Home page > 'Featured product — ZARAN' section > in Product, select your ZARAN 50ML product.
   This is what powers the real price and Add to Cart / Buy Now on the home page ZARAN card. (If you do not select it, the theme will automatically pick the first product and the prices will fall back to Rs. 2,499.00 / Rs. 3,999.00.)
3) CREATE THE 9 PAGES — the footer links (Shipping Policy, Return & Refund Policy, Exchange Policy, Cancellation Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, FAQ) only work after you create these pages once. Each page takes under a minute. For EVERY page in the list below, do exactly this:
   Shopify admin > Online Store > Pages > click 'Add page' > in Title, type the page title exactly as listed > leave the Content box EMPTY (the full page text is already baked into the theme template) > on the right side, under 'Theme template', select the template listed for that page > keep Visibility = Visible > click 'Save'.
   Typing the title creates the URL handle automatically — it must match the handle listed. (If it ever differs, fix it under 'Search engine listing' > Edit > URL handle.)
   - Title: Shipping Policy       handle: shipping-policy       template: page.shipping
   - Title: Return & Refund Policy handle: return-refund-policy template: page.returns
   - Title: Exchange Policy       handle: exchange-policy       template: page.exchange
   - Title: Cancellation Policy   handle: cancellation-policy   template: page.cancellation
   - Title: Privacy Policy        handle: privacy-policy        template: page.privacy
   - Title: Terms of Service      handle: terms-of-service      template: page.terms
   - Title: FAQ                   handle: faq                   template: page.faq
   - Title: About Us              handle: about-us              template: page.about
   - Title: Contact               handle: contact               template: page.contact
   Done — all 7 footer policy links, the footer FAQ link and the search PAGES results now open these pages with their full text.
4) CHECKOUT / COD: In Settings > Payments, turn on Cash on Delivery (Manual payment); in Settings > Shipping, set your delivery rates (create the FREE over Rs. 4,000 rule there).

HEADER NAV: The theme header tabs are the preview's FIXED 6 — HOME, SHOP ALL, REVIEWS, WHY FRAGAL, FAQ, CONTACT US (anchors #home, #shop, #reviews, #about, #faq, #contact). They are NOT built from any Shopify menu (Main menu or any other), so there is nothing to set in Content > Menus — Shopify's default menu (Home / Catalog / Contact) will not appear. Whichever section is open, that tab is highlighted with a gold color + underline (scroll-spy); the mobile drawer (right slide-in) has the same 6 links + socials.

SEARCH: The header search icon opens a black/gold panel — it opens empty; as you type, PRODUCTS (ZARAN) and PAGES (the 10 pages above) appear in two groups.

CART: This is not a demo — the cart drawer is connected to Shopify's AJAX Cart API (/cart/add.js, /cart.js, /cart/change.js). Both ADD TO CART and BUY NOW add the item and open the drawer; the header cart badge updates itself; the Checkout button goes to /checkout. On an empty cart, only 'Your cart is currently empty.' is shown.
